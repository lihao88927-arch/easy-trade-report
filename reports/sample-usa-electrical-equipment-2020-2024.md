## Methodology and limitations / 方法与局限

1. The target country is the **reporter** and trade flow is **imports** (`flowCode=M`). 目标国家作为报告国，统计进口流向。
2. Annual HS data are requested from UN Comtrade with the official `comtradeapicall` client. 本项目没有自行实现 HTTP API 客户端。
3. Import trend uses the UN Comtrade World aggregate where available; supplier rankings exclude partner code `0` (World) to avoid double counting.
4. Latest-year HHI, CR3, and CR5 use bilateral supplier values. Differences between their sum and the World total can arise from confidential, special-category, rounding, or reporting records.
5. Partner labels such as `Other Asia, nes` are reproduced exactly as published by UN Comtrade; the engine does not infer a country behind an aggregate or unspecified partner label.
6. UN Comtrade reflects reporter-submitted official statistics. Recent years may be revised, and country reporting schedules differ. Use the report for screening and content research, then validate high-stakes commercial decisions with current primary sources.
